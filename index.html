Omagenda

Privacy Terms GitHub

Omagenda is a plugin for the Omarchy Linux desktop. It shows the next calendar event in the desktop top bar, a seven-day agenda panel, and a Quick Add box that turns a typed sentence into an event. It is free and open source under the MIT license, published under the Fixed Supply name.

Omagenda asks for Google Calendar access so it can show your own events on your own computer and create, change, or delete events when you ask it to. It runs only on your machine; there is no Omagenda server.

Omagenda is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Google, Apple, or Flexibits.
